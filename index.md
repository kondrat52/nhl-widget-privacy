# Privacy Policy — NHL: Widget & Games

Last updated: 2026-09-27

## Summary

This app does not collect, store, or share personal data.

## What the app stores on your device

- Your favorite teams.
- A cache of recent game data, standings, and playoff bracket data.
- A cache of team logo images.

This data stays on your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Network access

The app connects to two public NHL servers:

- `api-web.nhle.com`, for scores, schedules, standings, and the playoff bracket.
- `assets.nhle.com`, for team logos.

The app sends no identifier, account, or personal data with these requests. As with any web request, these servers receive your IP address. The developer of this app receives no data.

## Permissions

- **Internet**: to download scores.
- **Run at startup**: to restart score updates after a reboot.
- **Exact alarms**: to refresh the widget every 60 seconds during live games. You grant this in system settings. It is optional.
- **Ignore battery optimizations**: to keep the widget current when the phone is idle. You grant this in system settings. It is optional.

## Analytics and advertising

The app has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs.

## Accounts

The app has no accounts and no sign-in.

## Children

The app does not target children and collects no data from any user.

## Affiliation

This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the National Hockey League or any NHL team. Team names and logos are trademarks of their owners and are used only to identify teams.
